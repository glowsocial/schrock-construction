About Us

Building Excellence
Since 2007

Schrock Construction has served Kitsap County and surrounding areas with professional remodeling, repair, and handyman services for nearly two decades.

Founded by Phillip Schrock, our business was built on a simple promise: deliver quality craftsmanship with honest, transparent service. As a licensed, bonded, and insured contractor, we hold ourselves to the highest standards on every project — big or small.

We believe your home deserves the best. That's why we stand behind our work with a comprehensive warranty, and why our clients continue to trust us year after year.
